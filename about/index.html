About WRS

A boutique penetration testing and vulnerability research firm. We find the flaws that off-the-shelf scanners miss, and explain how to fix them.

Who We Are

White Rabbit Security combines hands-on manual testing with automation we build ourselves. Scanners are useful for coverage, but the findings that matter most usually come from someone who understands how the system is supposed to work and then asks how it breaks.

WRS was founded on a simple premise: complex systems need cross-disciplinary thinking. Our approach draws on both physical hardware work and digital security testing, so we look at the whole attack surface, from embedded devices and bare-metal Linux to cloud-hosted applications and APIs.

Our Edge

Manual-First Testing

Testing is done by hand with proven tools such as Burp Suite, Nmap, and NetExec. Where source code is in scope, we write custom Semgrep rules for your stack. Every finding is confirmed manually before it reaches your report.

Hardware to Cloud

From board-level and embedded device security to exposed services and unauthenticated endpoints, we understand how each layer of a system can be reached and abused.

Diagnostic Rigor

Years of troubleshooting production networks and systems shape how we test: isolate the fault, prove it, and trace it back to the cause so the fix holds.

About the Founder

n30, Founder · OSCP, CompTIA Security+

I came to offensive security from the operations side. Before founding White Rabbit Security, I ran IT for an industrial business: the network, IP camera systems, endpoints, and helpdesk that kept it running. Being the person responsible for defending that network is what led me to attacking networks professionally.

That background shapes how I test. I approach every system the way I approached production problems: measure, isolate, prove the root cause, and explain the fix in plain terms. You don’t get raw scanner output from me. Every finding is confirmed by hand, reproduced, and written up so your team can act on it.

I also work below the software layer, with micro-soldering, PCB design, and embedded hardware, which helps when an assessment includes physical devices.
