About WRS

A boutique penetration testing and vulnerability research firm. We find the flaws that off-the-shelf scanners miss, and explain how to fix them.

Who We Are

White Rabbit Security combines hands-on manual testing with automation we build ourselves. Scanners are useful for coverage, but the findings that matter most usually come from someone who understands how the system is supposed to work and then asks how it breaks.

WRS was founded on a simple premise: complex systems need cross-disciplinary thinking. Our approach draws on both physical hardware work and digital security testing, so we look at the whole attack surface, from embedded devices and bare-metal Linux to cloud-hosted applications and APIs.

Our Edge

Custom Tooling

Beyond standard scripts, we build static and dynamic application testing (SAST/DAST) harnesses, including AI-assisted ones, tailored to your stack, then verify every result by hand before it reaches your report.

Hardware to Cloud

From board-level and embedded device security to exposed services and unauthenticated endpoints, we understand how each layer of a system can be reached and abused.

Diagnostic Rigor

Years of root-cause work on industrial equipment and IT infrastructure shape how we test: isolate the fault, prove it, and trace it back to the cause so the fix holds.

About the Founder

n30, Founder and Lead Researcher

Security isn’t just about code; it’s about understanding how complex systems break. The foundation of White Rabbit Security wasn’t built in a traditional IT classroom. It was built in the field, diagnosing failures in heavy industrial machinery, hydraulic systems, and three-phase electrical networks.

Years of isolating root causes on multi-ton equipment and micro-soldering custom hardware produce a disciplined diagnostic method. WRS applies that same first-principles approach to digital infrastructure. Whether it means building AI-assisted testing harnesses, tracing a flaw through an application’s logic, or exploiting a network misconfiguration, we don’t stop at what an automated scanner reports. We work through the system’s logic, find the weak points, and show you how to rebuild it stronger.

From hardware-level bus diagnostics to local, agent-driven AI testing environments, WRS bridges the gap between physical systems and digital security.
