How It Works

You approve a quote before any parts are ordered or repair work starts.

  1. Contact us

    Use the contact form to describe the device, the symptoms, and anything that has already been tried.

  2. Quote and drop-off

    We reply with any questions, an estimate, and local drop-off details.

  3. Diagnosis

    We test the device to find the root cause and confirm the price and parts before going further.

  4. Repair

    The approved repair is carried out, from connector and power faults to board-level work.

  5. Test and pickup

    The device is tested under normal use before it is ready for pickup.

Typical Timelines

These are typical ranges. Your quote gives the estimate for your specific job.

  • Diagnosis: 1 to 3 business days after drop-off.
  • Common repairs: usually 3 to 7 business days, such as ports, power faults, and board-level fixes.
  • Parts on order: add the shipping time for the part, which we confirm in the quote.
  • Custom builds and fabrication: scheduled per project.

Before You Drop Off

  • Back up if you can. If the device still turns on, copy off anything important first.
  • Bring the charger. Include the power supply and any cables or accessories needed to test the device.
  • Describe the fault. Note when the problem started and what triggers it. Intermittent faults are easier to find with details.
  • Keep passwords off the website. If a login is needed for testing, we arrange that in person, never through the contact form.
