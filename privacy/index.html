Privacy Notice

What this website collects, who processes it, and how to reach us about it.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What we collect

The contact form asks for your name, email address, the service you are interested in, and your message. Company and timeline are optional. We use this information only to reply to your inquiry and, if you engage us, to scope and run that engagement.

This site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies.

Services that process data for this site

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like most web hosts, it may record technical request data such as IP addresses.
  • Formspree receives contact form submissions and delivers them to us.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile runs the spam check on the contact form. It evaluates browser signals to tell people from automated traffic.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) serves the icon font used across the site.
  • Hacker News: the News page loads stories directly from the public Hacker News API in your browser.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

What not to send

Do not send passwords, API keys, or other sensitive data through this website. If an engagement needs credentials or confidential material, we agree a suitable channel in writing first. If you send something sensitive by mistake, tell us and we will delete it.

Retention and your choices

We keep inquiries for as long as we need them to respond and to keep ordinary business records. You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information you sent by contacting us through the contact page.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have found a vulnerability in this website, please tell us through the contact page, choosing “Other” as the service. Describe the issue in general terms first, without exploit payloads or sensitive data, and we will reply with a channel for the details. Please test only in ways that do not affect other visitors or the availability of the site. Our contact details for this are also published in security.txt.
